ASoC: amd: acp3x-es83xx: Fix headphone GPIO polarity on BOM-WXX9

The headphone-enable GPIO of the ES8336 codec is wired active-low on the
HUAWEI MateBook 14/15 2021 AMD boards (DMI board vendor HUAWEI, product
name BOM-WXX9, product version M1010): the codec output only reaches the
3.5 mm jack while that line is driven low.

The machine driver configures the line as active-high unconditionally, so
plugging headphones leaves the analog output muted. The jack is detected,
userspace switches to the headphone sink and plays, but nothing is heard
on the jack. Inserting the plug only halfway - so that the presence
switch is not asserted yet - does produce sound, which confirms the
inverted polarity.

Add an ES83XX_HP_LOW quirk and enable it for the BOM-WXX9/M1010 DMI entry
so that the GPIO is driven low while the headphone path is powered up.
With the correct polarity the speakers also mute properly as soon as
headphones are plugged in.

Tested on a MateBook 14 2021 (BOM-WXX9-PCB-B2): dmesg now reports
"headphone gpio 1 active low" and audio is audible through the jack.
